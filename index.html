01 Why Phuket, and not the others

Four Thai options were priced for the same dates. The deciding factor for a three-night trip is not the destination — it is how much of the trip you lose to flying.

OptionBest fare found
(return, per person)		Fastest routingVerdict
Phuket (HKT)Chosen ₹41,734 Non-stop, 4h 50m Beach Christmas, shortest travel, one hotel for all three nights.
Bangkok (BKK) ₹40,090 Non-stop, 4h 05m Cheapest and easiest, but a city break — no beach, and Bangkok has had serious flooding this season.
Krabi (KBV) ₹55,830 1 stop, 10h+ No non-stop from Delhi; you lose most of day one in transit.
Bangkok + Phuket split ₹55,000+ Non-stop + domestic hop Two hotels, one wasted half-day packing, and a domestic flight on peak dates.
The reasoning in one line: a 3-night trip has roughly two usable days. Phuket is the only one of these that gives you a beach at the end of a single non-stop flight — Bangkok saves about ₹1,600 and costs you the sea, Krabi costs ₹14,000 and a whole day.

03 Where to stay

Three nights, two adults, 24–27 December. Prices are totals for the whole stay, not per night. Source: Booking.com, captured 17 Oct 2026.

Kata — the pick

Two beaches (Kata and quieter Kata Noi), walkable restaurants, 15 minutes to the Big Buddha and 25 to Promthep Cape. Calmer than Patong, and the right base for a 3-day trip.

Phuket Town

Cheapest beds (from ₹12,791 for three nights) and the Old Town on your doorstep, but a 30–40 minute drive to any beach. Only worth it if you are treating this as a food-and-culture trip.

Patong

Nightlife and the busiest beach; also the most expensive and loudest. Skip it for a Christmas trip unless the point of the trip is Bangla Road.

04 The three days

Built around the SpiceJet timings, which give you two full days plus a beach morning. Everything is within 40 minutes of Kata, so no day is wasted driving.

  1. Day 1 · Thu 24 Dec

    Land, drop bags, sunset

    Christmas Eve
    • 10:30
      Depart Delhi — SpiceJet non-stop, 4h 50m.
    • 16:40
      Land at Phuket (HKT). Immigration is quick with the visa exemption; eSIM on before you leave the hall.
    • 17:00
      Private transfer to Kata — 60–75 min, about 1,100–1,200 THB. Pre-book rather than queue for a meter taxi.
    • 18:20
      Sunset on Kata Beach — the sun drops around 18:20 in late December.
    • 20:00
      Christmas Eve dinner at a beachfront table; book ahead, the good ones sell out this week.
  2. Day 2 · Fri 25 Dec

    Phi Phi, Maya Bay and Bamboo Island

    Christmas Day
    • 07:00
      Hotel pickup for the speedboat day trip — the operator collects from Kata.
    • 09:00
      Maya Bay, the beach from The Beach. Landings are regulated and time-limited; go early, it is the whole reason for the early start.
    • 11:00
      Pileh Lagoon and Loh Samah Bay for snorkelling, then Viking Cave.
    • 12:30
      Lunch on Phi Phi Don, then beach time.
    • 14:30
      Bamboo Island — the quietest stop of the day.
    • 16:30
      Back at the hotel. Shower, then Kata night market for Christmas dinner — cheap, excellent, no booking.
  3. Day 3 · Sat 26 Dec

    Big Buddha, cape sunset, Old Town

    • 08:00
      Kata Noi for a quiet swim before the heat and the crowds.
    • 10:30
      Big Buddha — the 45m white statue on Nakkerd Hill. Free; dress covered, shoulders and knees.
    • 12:00
      Wat Chalong, Phuket’s most important temple, 10 minutes away. Free.
    • 13:00
      Lunch and a spa hour back in Kata.
    • 17:15
      Promthep Cape for sunset — get there 45 minutes early for a spot at the rail.
    • 19:30
      Phuket Old Town — Thalang Road’s Sino-Portuguese shophouses, dinner and a walk down Soi Romanee.
  4. Day 4 · Sun 27 Dec

    Last swim, then home

    • 08:00
      Breakfast and a last beach hour.
    • 12:00
      Check out, leave bags at reception.
    • 14:30
      Transfer to the airport — allow 75 minutes plus the two-hour check-in buffer.
    • 17:40
      Fly to Delhi, landing 21:00.
If the flight moves to the 07:35 Air India return, drop the last beach morning and leave Kata at 04:45 — which is a strong argument for paying for the afternoon SpiceJet return instead.

05 Sightseeing

What is actually worth the time on a three-day trip, and what each one costs.

Phi Phi & Maya Bay

3,390 THB per adult · ~₹9,300

Full-day speedboat: Maya Bay, Pileh Lagoon, Loh Samah, Viking Cave, Phi Phi Don and Bamboo Island. Includes hotel transfer, lunch, snorkelling gear and insurance. National park fee of 400 THB is extra, in cash on the day.

07:00–08:00 pickup, back about 16:30 · book ahead for Christmas Day

Big Buddha

Free donations welcome

A 45-metre white marble Buddha on the highest hill in the south, with a 360° view over Chalong Bay and Kata. Go before 11:00 — it is exposed, and there is no shade on the platform.

6:00–18:30 daily · 30 min from Kata · cover shoulders and knees

Promthep Cape

Free parking ~50 THB

The southern tip of the island and the standard sunset shot. Busy but genuinely spectacular; walk past the lighthouse for the quieter rail.

Sunset ~18:15 in late December · arrive 17:15

Phuket Old Town

Free dinner ~₹1,500 for two

Tin-mining money built these Sino-Portuguese shophouses. Thalang Road, Soi Romanee and the cafés around Dibuk Road are the walk. The Sunday walking street runs 16:00–22:00 — which this itinerary misses by one day.

Best 17:00–22:00 · 35 min from Kata

Wat Chalong

Free donations

The largest and most visited temple on the island, three storeys of murals and a revered relic. Ten minutes from the Big Buddha, so it pairs naturally.

8:00–17:00 · 20 min from Kata

Kata & Kata Noi beaches

Free sunbeds ~200 THB

The reason to stay here. Kata is the long curve with the island view; Kata Noi, over the headland, is smaller and quieter. Watch the flags — there are real rip currents in December.

Walk from most Kata hotels

07 Practical

Before you fly

  • Visa: Indian passports enter Thailand visa-free for up to 30 days. The stay limit was cut from 60 days to 30 on 15 September 2026 — still no visa, no fee, for a four-day trip.
  • Passport: six months validity and two blank pages. Carry a printed hotel booking and the return ticket.
  • Money: cards work in hotels and malls; markets, boats and taxis want cash. ATMs charge about 220 THB per withdrawal, so take a larger draw, or change cash at SuperRich.
  • eSIM: an 8-day tourist eSIM is about ₹500 and beats airport SIM queues.
  • Insurance: the boat operator’s cover is basic. A short trip policy is roughly ₹800 for two.

On the ground

  • Weather: December is the dry season on the Andaman side — 27–32°C, low rain, very high UV. This is the correct coast to be on in December; the Gulf islands are the ones that get wet.
  • Transfers: Grab works in Phuket; a private airport car to Kata is 1,100–1,200 THB. Pre-book for the arrival — the taxi touts at HKT quote double.
  • Water safety: red flags mean rip currents, and they are not decorative. Kata and Kata Noi both have lifeguards in season.
  • Temple etiquette: shoulders and knees covered, shoes off, no pointing feet at the Buddha.
  • Christmas week: book the hotel and the boat before the flight, in that order — the good Kata properties and the reputable boat operators both sell out.
